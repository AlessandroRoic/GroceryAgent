# Grocery planning

The food available at home, the people eating it, and the meals and groceries planned for the week.

## Language

**Household**:
The group of people whose groceries and meals are planned together using a shared inventory.

**Food profile**:
A household member's hard exclusions and food preferences.

**Routine**:
The household's reusable pattern for a typical week. It supplies default values for who eats at home, what kinds of meals they want, and how much time they have to cook.
_Avoid_: Default routine

**Weekly plan**:
The household's choices for breakfasts, lunches, and dinners in a specific calendar week, including who is eating, what they plan to eat, and when they plan to eat out. It starts from the routine and can be adjusted for that week.
_Avoid_: Weekly meal plan, plan (when the reference is ambiguous)

**Weekly override**:
A change to a routine default for one weekly plan, such as switching Tuesday lunch from eating at home to eating at work.

**Planning priorities**:
The household's adjustable goals for a weekly plan, such as reducing food waste or varying meals. These goals do not override anyone's hard exclusions.

**Diet guidance**:
General eating goals and culinary preferences that guide meal suggestions, such as Italian cooking, eating for fitness, or avoiding particular ingredient combinations. Guidance is flexible unless a requirement is explicitly recorded as a hard exclusion.

**Shared meal**:
A meal intended for multiple household members. It respects every participant's hard exclusions and normally avoids their dislikes unless an exception is approved.

**Leftovers**:
Prepared food remaining from an earlier meal that may be eaten at a later meal, without a fixed next-day commitment.

**Eating out**:
A meal occasion when a household member plans to get their meal elsewhere, such as lunch provided at work.

**Inventory**:
The approximate record of food believed to be available at home and its quantities, which may be incomplete until updated.
_Avoid_: Pantry (when referring to all food at home)

**Inventory check**:
A review of the food actually available at home to correct the inventory.
_Avoid_: Stock check

**Shopping review**:
A check of the groceries actually bought and their quantities at the end of a shopping trip, before those purchases are added to inventory.

**Use soon**:
A priority assigned to food that the household wants to use before other available food.

**Use-by date**:
The date on a food package that relates to food safety when the stated storage instructions are followed.

**Best-before date**:
The date on a food package through which the food is expected to retain its quality when stored as directed.

**Estimated date**:
A suggested date based on available product information, marked as an AI estimate until checked against the package.

**After-opening guidance**:
Instructions for how long to keep a product after opening, based on the package instructions when available.

**Receipt**:
A record of groceries bought on a shopping trip, used to identify additions to the inventory.

**Shopping list**:
The groceries intended to be bought to support the weekly plan.

**Recipe**:
A reusable description of a meal's ingredients, quantities, cooking instructions, and serving yield.

**Recipe library**:
The household's saved collection of recipes, including regular meals and recipes added for future use.
_Avoid_: Dictionary, cooking book

**Receipt draft**:
A proposed interpretation of a receipt's grocery items and quantities, including unresolved uncertainties, awaiting review before inclusion in inventory.

**Hard exclusion**:
A food or ingredient a person will not eat and that must be excluded from meals intended for that person.
_Avoid_: Preference (when exclusion is mandatory)

**Preference**:
A person's food likes or dislikes that guide meal choices without making a food strictly forbidden.
_Avoid_: Hard exclusion (when a choice is negotiable)
